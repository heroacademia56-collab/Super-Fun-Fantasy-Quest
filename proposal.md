## Description
A fantasy-style interactive mini game where the player embarks on a branching quest to find the magical Sword of Power and save the kingdom from an evil monster. The game blends narrative choice-making with visual storytelling, relevant to digital arts through its use of animation, character design, and interactive media.

## Features
- **Branching Narrative System**
  - Players make choices that affect the story path, using a simple decision tree structure.
- **Hero & Monster Character Design**
  - Stylized 2D or 3D models created in Blender or Maya, with symbolic traits tied to the story.
- **Sword of Power Mechanic**
  - Unlockable sword with visual effects and stat boosts, triggered by quest completion.
- **Interactive UI**
  - Dialogue boxes, choice buttons, and inventory system built with Python (e.g., Pygame or tkinter).
- **Victory & Failure Endings**
  - Multiple endings based on player decisions, animated with visual effects and music cues.

## Challenges
- Learning how to implement branching logic and state tracking in Python.
- Designing and animating characters and magical effects in Blender or Maya.
- Creating a responsive and visually appealing user interface.
- Balancing narrative pacing with gameplay mechanics.
